About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at UC Irvine, where I work under the supervision of Professor Lee Swindlehurst{% include newtab.html %}. My research centers on advancing wireless communication systems through signal processing, optimization techniques, AI/machine learning, and hands-on experimental implementations.

Research interests

  • Wireless communication systems
  • Integrated sensing and communication
  • Cell-free and distributed massive MIMO
  • AI and machine learning for wireless networks
  • Distributed and decentralized optimization
