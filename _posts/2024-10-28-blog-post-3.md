For the second year in a row, I attended the Asilomar Conference on Signals, Systems, and Computers (2023 and 2024).

This year, I gave a lecture on my work on consensus-ADMM optimization for beamforming in cell-free systems.
It was a pleasure reconnecting with Dr. César A. Uribe, and I’m excited about the potential for future collaborations.
I would also like to acknowledge the invaluable support of my current advisor, Prof. Lee Swindlehurst, whose guidance has helped me build and maintain connections with colleagues and collaborators in the field.
