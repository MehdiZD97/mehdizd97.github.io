I was collecting extensive datasets for the experimental phase of my current project, "Evaluating Distributed MU-MIMO Beamforming."
We used the [RENEW](https://renew-wireless.org/) programmable massive MIMO platform: four radio heads, each with a linear array of 16 antennas, connected to a central hub as our access points (APs), and six standalone Iris SDRs as our clients (UEs).
We collected uplink (pilot and data) datasets in multiple scenarios:

- Centralized: co-located APs with coherent or non-coherent arrays
- Distributed: distributed APs with coherent or non-coherent arrays

We made these datasets publicly available for research purposes; they are also on IEEE DataPort as the [Distributed Multi-User MIMO Datasets]({{ '/publications/distributed-mu-mimo-datasets/' | relative_url }}).
In our previous work, published at [IEEE SAM 2022]({{ '/publications/robustness-distributed-beamforming/' | relative_url }}), we generated extensive datasets that are also available.
