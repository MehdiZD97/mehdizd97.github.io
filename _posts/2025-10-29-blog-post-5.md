This year, my paper "**Coordinated Decentralized Resource Optimization for Cell-Free ISAC Systems**" was shortlisted for the Best Paper Award.
I presented a poster in the Student Paper Contest (SPC) and gave a lecture at the conference.
It was a pleasure having my advisor, Prof. Lee Swindlehurst, attend my lecture and support my presentation.
