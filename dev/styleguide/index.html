Design directions

Direction B (Cooperative) is the main style (light by default); Direction A (Instrument) is developed in parallel so the site can switch styles easily. Both pages share the same markup, hero, and scripts; only colors, type, and shapes differ.

The pages follow the proposed home page: the hero (two switchable views, cell-free first), then News, Publications (journal and conference groups), CV (education, experience, honors and awards, teaching, talks), and Videos. The header stays at the top and its underline follows the section in view.

These pages are for development only: hidden from search engines, and kept out of the live site's build from Stage 3 on.
